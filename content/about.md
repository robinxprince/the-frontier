## Why this exists
THE FRONTIER follows discoveries, open problems and serious debates across computing, economics, science, governance, energy, neuroscience and philosophy. Unexpected discoveries are part of the remit, not an afterthought.

Each research edition aims to explain the problem, the mechanism, the evidence, the limitations and what to watch next. Cross-disciplinary connections should expose a causal relationship, not merely put two fashionable ideas beside each other.

## How editions are made
Research and writing use a scheduled assistant in the publisher's existing desktop app. The intended start time is 7:00 AM Asia/Kolkata. Publication follows research and checks, so this is not a guaranteed delivery time. The local computer must be awake, connected and signed in. Usage limits or access failures can delay an edition.

No paid research API is required. GitHub Actions builds and deploys the static site after an edition is committed. A separate daily check records whether an edition exists; it does not invent one when the researcher is unavailable.

## Read the evidence
Research briefings are AI-assisted. Primary papers, institutional documents and verified expert explanations take priority. Source links support inspection; a working link alone does not prove a claim. Publication dates, evidence status and unresolved questions should stay close to the claims they qualify.

Preprints are labelled as preprints. Scenarios are not predictions. Legal and economic interpretations should distinguish facts, assumptions and disputed conclusions. An unavailable or unverified source is a reason to omit a claim, not to fill the gap from memory.

## Learn beyond headlines
The learning section favours researchers explaining their own work, engineers demonstrating real systems and educators who make their reasoning visible. Daily editions should include verified videos where good ones exist, or clearly identified lecture libraries when a specific video cannot be verified.

## An archive you can keep
Every edition is a Markdown file in the [public repository](https://github.com/robinxprince/the-frontier). Its dated address stays stable. Edits remain visible in Git history, and each edition has a Markdown download. Keeping a local clone provides an additional backup. Availability depends on retaining the repository and hosting account.

## Corrections
[Open an issue](https://github.com/robinxprince/the-frontier/issues/new) with the claim, source and proposed correction. Substantive corrections should be dated in the edition itself. A correction must not silently rewrite what an earlier edition asserted.
